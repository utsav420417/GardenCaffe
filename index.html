About Us

The Garden Café sits in an open area away from busy residential streets, so it stays quiet and calm. Tables sit on gravel in the shade of old trees, prayer flags hang overhead, and the rice fields are just beyond the fence.

Come for a cup of tea, a plate of momo or a long afternoon with friends. Sit in the open garden or under the covered area.

  • Open-air garden seating
  • Covered area for sun and rain
  • Food, snacks and hot drinks
  • Home delivery in town
  • Order ahead on WhatsApp
